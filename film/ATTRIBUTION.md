# Film pipeline — third-party notices

The demo film is composed from this folder and never shipped as part of the app.

- HyperFrames (HTML compositions rendered with Puppeteer and FFmpeg) — see film/node_modules/hyperframes/LICENSE after `npm install`.
- Playwright (Apache-2.0) — product footage recorded on the live app.
- Remotion — the two typography shots (film/remotion); used under Remotion's free license for individuals.
- three.js (MIT), bundled into film/comp/assets/knot.js for the knot.
- p5.js (LGPL-2.1), vendored as film/comp/assets/vendor/p5.min.js for the watercolor opening.
- GSAP 3.15.0 (GSAP Standard License, free for all uses), vendored as film/comp/assets/vendor/gsap.min.js.
- Fonts: Space Grotesk and JetBrains Mono (SIL Open Font License), copied from public/fonts.
- Voice: the owner's own voice, cloned with his consent (Higgsfield voice clone) and rendered with MiniMax text-to-speech.
- Music: an instrumental bed generated with vidIQ's music tool for this video.
- Sound effects: synthesized with FFmpeg; no sample packs.
- Photos in the film: the app's seed photos (see public/seed/ATTRIBUTION.md) and the owner's own.
